## ✨ Features & Sections

### 1. Divine Hero Section
- Invocation: `|| श्री गणेशाय नमः || श्री राधा-कृष्णाय नमः ||`
- Calligraphy typography: `Hemanth weds Yashaswini` with script flourishes.
- Spaced wedding date: `0 1  N O V E M B E R  2 0 2 6`.
- 3D Radha Krishna illustration with radiant radial halo glow.
- Auspicious Sanskrit shloka with English translation.
- Live countdown timer (Days, Hours, Minutes, Seconds).

### 2. Meet The Bride & Groom
- **Bride Card**: Features playful baby Krishna peeking in from the left screen boundary, photo frame card with "Bride Pic" placeholder, bride's lineage, and bio.
- **Groom Card**: Features playful baby Radha peeking in from the right screen boundary, photo frame card with "Groom Pic" placeholder, groom's lineage, and bio.
- **Interactive Photo Upload**: Click "Change Photo" on either card to replace portraits on the fly!

### 3. Interactive Scratch to Reveal
- Faithful reproduction of the royal blue scratch box from the reference template.
- Built on HTML5 Canvas: Guests can scratch off the royal blue foil with their finger (mobile) or mouse cursor (desktop).
- Revealing >40% triggers a burst of golden confetti, audio chime, and unveils the auspicious Muhurtham (10:24 AM) & gift pass code (`#RADHE-SHYAM-2026`).
- Includes a "Quick Reveal" button for convenience.

### 4. Order of Events (Timeline)
- Three ceremony cards utilizing the high-definition custom artwork banners:
  1. **Wedding (Muhurtham)**: Vedic Vivaah ceremony card with date, time, and venue.
  2. **The Grand Reception**: Evening gala card.
  3. **Haldi & Mehendi**: Sun-kissed turmeric and henna celebration.
- Every event card features:
  - "Add to Calendar" (.ics calendar file generation).
  - "Directions" button opening interactive venue maps.

### 5. Interactive RSVP Section
- Styled in the royal blue theme from the reference template.
- Full interactive form:
  - Guest Name & Phone / WhatsApp.
  - Attendance Status (Joyfully Accept / Regretfully Decline).
  - Guest Count selector (1 to 5+).
  - Event checkmarks (Haldi, Wedding, Reception).
  - Personal blessings text area.
- Instant submission with confetti fireworks, success toast, and persistence in `localStorage`.

### 6. Kindly Join Us (Family Blessings)
- Radha Krishna on the sacred swing (Jhula) featuring realistic pendulum swaying physics.
- Heartfelt invitation verse:
  > *"With hearts full of joy, we invite you to celebrate our special day. Your presence and blessings will make our celebration truly memorable."*
- Host lines: **Raja Vardhan with Meera Devi**.
- Closing tagline: `WE LOOK FORWARD TO CELEBRATING WITH YOU`.
- Quick action pills: WhatsApp invitation sharing, link copy, and print/PDF.

### 7. Animations & Effects
- **Text Blur Entrance**: As sections scroll into view, text transitions from `blur(10px)` to crisp `blur(0px)` with upward motion.
- **Hover Micro-interactions**: 3D card tilt, golden glow highlights, and peeking character bobbing/wiggling.
- **Ambient Petals**: Floating rose petals and gold glitter drift across the screen.
- **Web Audio Bansuri Flute**: Native browser synthesizer generating a serene Raag Yaman flute melody without any external audio files or broken links.
- **Screen View Toggle**: Allows toggling between full laptop screen view and an iPhone phone preview simulation frame.
- **Live Customizer**: Click the "Customize" button on the top toolbar to edit names, dates, hosts, and venues in real time.
